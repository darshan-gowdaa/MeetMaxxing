MEETMAXXING CHROME EXTENSION - SETUP INSTRUCTIONS
===================================================

Follow these steps to set up MeetMaxxing in Google Chrome, Brave, or Microsoft Edge.

STEP 1: UNPACK
Extract all files from meetmaxxing-chrome.zip into a permanent folder on your computer (for example: Documents/MeetMaxxing-Chrome). Do not delete or move this folder after installing.

STEP 2: OPEN EXTENSIONS PAGE
In Google Chrome address bar, type:
chrome://extensions
and press Enter. (In Edge use edge://extensions, in Brave use brave://extensions)

STEP 3: ENABLE DEVELOPER MODE
In the top right corner of the Extensions page, switch the "Developer mode" toggle ON.

STEP 4: LOAD UNPACKED EXTENSION
Click the "Load unpacked" button in the top left corner.
In the file picker, select the folder where you unzipped meetmaxxing-chrome.zip (the folder with manifest.json inside).

STEP 5: PIN EXTENSION
Click the puzzle piece icon on your browser toolbar near your profile picture, find "MeetMaxxing", and click the Pin icon so it stays visible.

STEP 6: LOGIN & USE
1. Go to https://meetmaxxing.vercel.app and log in to your account.
2. Join any meeting on https://meet.google.com.
3. Turn on Captions (press 'c' key in Google Meet).
4. The MeetMaxxing sidebar will appear on the right side with real-time tips, action items, and live summaries.
