# MeetMaxxing — Chrome Extension Setup & Guide

MeetMaxxing is an AI copilot for Google Meet. It sits right inside your meeting window, tracks conversation captions in real time, flags action items, and generates summaries using Gemini.

## Quick Install (Chrome / Brave / Edge)

1. Download and unzip `meetmaxxing-chrome.zip` to a folder on your computer. Keep the folder somewhere safe (don't delete it after installing, or Chrome won't find it).
2. Open Google Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions`.
3. Turn on the **Developer mode** toggle in the top right corner.
4. Click the **Load unpacked** button in the top left.
5. Select the unzipped folder (the folder containing `manifest.json`).
6. You will see "MeetMaxxing — AI Meeting Copilot" in your extensions list. Click the puzzle icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin MeetMaxxing so it's always visible.

## How to Use

1. Log in: Click the MeetMaxxing extension icon in your toolbar, or visit https://meetmaxxing.vercel.app to log into your account.
2. Join any Google Meet (https://meet.google.com).
3. The MeetMaxxing floating sidebar will automatically appear on the right side of your meeting.
4. Turn on Captions in Google Meet (press `c` on your keyboard or click the CC icon on the Meet toolbar). MeetMaxxing uses live captions to follow the conversation and generate insights.
5. As people speak, you will see real-time suggestions, follow-up reminders, and key discussion points appear in the sidebar.
6. When your meeting ends, click End Meeting in the sidebar to review the full summary, action items, and follow-up email drafts on your web dashboard.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Does this record my camera or mic?
No. The extension does not record your screen or camera. It only reads the text captions generated by Google Meet's built-in live captioning service.

### Why do I need to turn on Captions (CC)?
MeetMaxxing parses the caption text stream from Google Meet to analyze the meeting in real-time. Without captions enabled, there is no text for the AI models to process.

### Can other people in the meeting see that I'm using MeetMaxxing?
No. The sidebar is injected only into your local browser window. Other participants cannot see it and Google Meet does not notify anyone.

### The first summary or request is taking a while. Why?
The backend API runs on a cloud server that sleeps when idle. The first request after a period of inactivity may take 30 to 45 seconds to wake up. Once awake, all queries and summaries are fast.

### Can I use my own Gemini or OpenAI API keys?
Yes! Head over to your Settings page on https://meetmaxxing.vercel.app/settings/api-keys to add your personal keys from Google, Groq, OpenRouter, or Perplexity.

## Troubleshooting

### The sidebar is not appearing in Google Meet
* Make sure you are in an active meeting room (URL looks like `meet.google.com/abc-defg-hij`), not just on the Google Meet homepage.
* Hard-refresh the tab (`Ctrl + F5` on Windows/Linux or `Cmd + Shift + R` on Mac).
* Open `chrome://extensions`, find MeetMaxxing, click the reload icon (circular arrow), and then refresh your Google Meet tab.

### "Failed to connect" or "Authentication required" error
* Open a new tab and visit https://meetmaxxing.vercel.app.
* Log out and log back in with your account.
* Go back to Google Meet and refresh the page. The extension will automatically pick up your refreshed session.

### Captions are on but sidebar says "Waiting for speaker..."
* Speak a few words or wait for someone else to speak so Google Meet starts rendering text on screen.
* Ensure Google Meet's caption language is set to English.

### How do I update to a newer version?
Download the latest `meetmaxxing-chrome.zip`, unzip and replace the files inside your existing extension folder, then go to `chrome://extensions` and click the reload icon on the MeetMaxxing card.
