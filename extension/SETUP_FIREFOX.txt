MEETMAXXING FIREFOX EXTENSION - SETUP GUIDE
============================================

OFFICIAL 1-CLICK INSTALL (RECOMMENDED):
If you just want to install and use MeetMaxxing permanently, use the official Mozilla Add-ons store link:
https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/meetmaxxing/

Click "Add to Firefox" on the page and you are good to go!

--------------------------------------------
MANUAL INSTALLATION FROM THIS ZIP (DEVELOPER / TESTING):
--------------------------------------------

1. EXTRACT FILES
Extract meetmaxxing-firefox.zip into any folder on your computer.

2. OPEN FIREFOX DEBUGGING
Open a new tab in Firefox and go to:
about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox

3. LOAD ADD-ON
Click the "Load Temporary Add-on..." button.
Browse into the unzipped folder and select the "manifest.json" file.

4. PIN EXTENSION
Click the puzzle piece icon on the right side of the Firefox navigation bar and click the gear/pin next to MeetMaxxing to keep it handy.

5. LOGIN AND TEST
- Log in to your account at https://meetmaxxing.vercel.app
- Join any meeting on https://meet.google.com
- Press 'c' to turn on captions in Google Meet
- The MeetMaxxing AI assistant sidebar will open on your right screen.
